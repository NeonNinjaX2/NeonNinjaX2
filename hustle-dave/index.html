Hustle Dave

Dave just turned 18. He has $1,500 and a bedroom at his mom's house. You get ten real years. Pick when his story starts: the stock market, gas prices, inflation, home prices and the news all follow what actually happened.

  • Get a job, then click Work hard during work hours to earn raises.
  • Rent, food, gas, taxes and loans come out automatically. Run out of cash and it goes on a 22% credit card.
  • School unlocks better jobs. Investing, a house and a car are up to you.
  • The score is Dave's net worth when the ten years end.
