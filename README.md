## 🎮 Hustle Dave

A life-sim money game. [Play it here](https://neonninjax2.github.io/NeonNinjaX2/hustle-dave/).

Dave is 18 with $1,500 and a room at his mom's. You get ten real years (start in 2000, 2008 or 2016) to make him rich.

- **Real history:** the S&P 500, gold, oil, home prices, interest rates and inflation follow real data. Real headlines happen on their real dates (the 2008 crash, COVID, stimulus checks, oil going negative).
- **Real life:** jobs at real median wages, rent, groceries, gas, taxes, health insurance, student loans, car loans, mortgages and a 22% credit card. Dave can go bankrupt.
- **A real game:** an animated street with a day/night cycle, seasons and weather. Dave walks or drives to work, and you click **Work hard** during his shifts to earn raises. There are also sound effects, life events with choices, goals and high scores.

Data: monthly S&P 500, CPI, gold, Case-Shiller home prices, 10-year Treasury yields, and daily WTI oil and VIX from the public [datasets](https://github.com/datasets) collection on GitHub.
